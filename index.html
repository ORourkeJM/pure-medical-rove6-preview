GET TO KNOW THE ROVE 6

A portable companion
for life on the go.

The Inogen Rove 6 is a portable oxygen concentrator that provides supplemental oxygen through pulse-dose delivery. Its rechargeable battery and compact design make it an option to discuss for outings away from home.

Lightweight design
4.8 lbs with the standard battery.
Six pulse-dose settings
Settings 1–6, as prescribed by your clinician.
Battery choices
Standard and extended battery options.
Straightforward controls
An easy-to-read display and simple controls.

Portable pulse-dose oxygen is different from continuous-flow home oxygen. Your clinician determines whether the Rove 6 meets your prescribed needs.

What is a portable oxygen concentrator?+

A portable oxygen concentrator is a rechargeable device designed to provide supplemental oxygen while you are on the go. The Rove 6 delivers oxygen in pulses when you inhale. It requires a prescription, and your clinician must determine whether it meets your oxygen needs.

Can I request the Rove 6 before I have a prescription?+

Yes. You can make your initial request and talk with our team before providing a prescription. A prescription is required before fulfillment.

Can it replace my home oxygen equipment?+

That depends on your prescription. The Rove 6 uses pulse-dose delivery, while many stationary concentrators provide continuous flow. Your clinician determines whether the Rove 6 is appropriate for you and how it fits into your oxygen plan.
