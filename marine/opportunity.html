THE OPENING

A procurement story.
Built on trust.

Recommended launch geography: Fort Lauderdale and Broward first, then Palm Beach and Miami. This is a test plan, not a measured ranking of demand.

01 · AUDIENCE

The decision chain.

Captains, yacht managers and authorized procurement staff. Start with professionally crewed private vessels; keep charter operations as a distinct segment.

02 · MESSAGE

The people aboard.

Guests, staff and crew. Present oxygen as part of a broader readiness plan, with equipment configuration, intended use and training reviewed for each vessel.

03 · CHANNELS

High intent. Local trust.

Test relevant search alongside yacht-management, provisioning, training and marina referral channels. These are candidate channels, not established partnerships.
