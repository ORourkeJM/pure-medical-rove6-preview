RESERVATION DETAILS

A few details.
Clearly considered.

What does the $2,500 payment cover?+

It is a $2,500 refundable deposit per vessel reservation, including a request for multiple kits. The final kit configuration, quantity, price and remaining balance are provided following the team review.

Can management request more than one kit?+

Yes. Select 1, 2, 3 or 4+ kits in the vessel form. The team confirms the final configuration and quantity during the review after deposit payment.

When does the team conversation happen?+

Following confirmed deposit payment. Completing the vessel form alone does not activate the team follow-up.

Is a shipping date guaranteed by the deposit?+

Your preferred delivery month begins the planning process. The team confirms an estimated shipping window after reviewing the kit configuration, fulfillment requirements and supply availability.

Is oxygen required for every international voyage?+

Requirements differ by flag, vessel operation, crew and voyage. Vessel-specific requirements and the appropriate kit configuration are reviewed as part of the fulfillment process.

What if the order cannot proceed?+

The reservation deposit is refundable. The final reservation terms will specify the refund process and timing before live payment collection begins.
